MORPHORA V4.9.8.1 — Hotfix Validation Notes

Issue addressed
- The Draw on image button could appear visually but fail to respond because drawing.js initialized before script.js had finished exposing window.MorphoraAtlas.

Expected result
- Clicking Draw on image after opening an anatomical view should activate drawing mode, show the drawing toolbar, hide labels temporarily, and allow pen/highlighter/eraser tools to work.

Recommended manual test
1. Hard-refresh the deployed page after pushing this build.
2. Open a dog skull view.
3. Click Draw on image.
4. Confirm the drawing toolbar appears.
5. Draw with Pen.
6. Switch to Highlighter and draw.
7. Use Eraser.
8. Click Done and confirm normal navigation returns.
9. Reload the page and confirm saved strokes reappear for the same anatomical view.
