# MORPHORA V4.8 Study & Quiz Test Checklist

## Explore mode
- Open every canine skull orientation.
- Confirm normal labels, descriptions, search and personal notes still work.
- Switch from Study or Quiz back to Explore and verify the previous label visibility is restored.

## Study mode
- Open Study mode from desktop controls and the mobile toolbar.
- Confirm labels hide immediately.
- Reveal and hide the current structure.
- Move Previous/Next and select items from the structure list.
- Reveal all, hide all and filter structures.
- Mark and unmark a structure for review.
- Refresh the browser and confirm review/mastery data remains stored.

## Locate quiz
- Start a 5-question Locate session.
- Click directly on an anchor, near it and far away.
- Confirm Correct, Close and Not quite feedback.
- Verify the correct structure is highlighted after every answer.
- Finish the session and inspect score, average time and mistakes.

## Multiple choice
- Start a session in a view with at least two eligible labels.
- Confirm the highlighted anchor has no visible name before answering.
- Answer with buttons and keyboard keys 1–4.
- Confirm correct/incorrect options are identified.

## Flashcards
- Reveal an answer using the button and Enter/Space.
- Rate each recall option.
- Confirm Again/Difficult marks a structure for review.
- Confirm Easy can remove a high-mastery structure from review.

## Progress
- Open Learning progress.
- Verify attempts, accuracy, mastery and recent sessions.
- Export progress and inspect the JSON.
- Import by merge and replace.
- Clear progress only after exporting a backup.

## Content Studio
- Select a label and edit quiz eligibility, difficulty and tolerance.
- Export JSON and confirm the three fields are present.
- Verify validation rejects tolerance outside 0.005–0.2.

## Responsive/accessibility
- Test desktop, tablet landscape and mobile portrait.
- Navigate drawers/dialogs with Tab and Shift+Tab.
- Close with Escape and confirm focus returns to the opening control.
- Test browser text zoom at 200% and reduced motion.
