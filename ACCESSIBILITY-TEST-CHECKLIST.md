# MORPHORA V4.6 Accessibility Test Checklist

## Keyboard
- Tab through header actions in a logical order.
- Open and close Library with Enter and Escape.
- Confirm focus remains trapped in open modal drawers/dialogs.
- Confirm focus returns to the opening button after Escape.
- Open global search with Ctrl/Cmd + K and `/`.
- Open labels and notes with Enter or Space.

## Mobile and touch
- Pinch and pan the image without accidental note creation.
- Drag notes without also opening them.
- Confirm mobile toolbar does not cover the navigator or information panel.
- Test portrait and landscape orientation.
- Test the Studio Library, Viewer and Inspector tabs.

## Visual access
- Test light and dark themes.
- Increase browser zoom to 200%.
- Enable reduced motion.
- Enable higher contrast / forced colors where available.
- Confirm active controls use more than color alone.

## Screen-reader status
- Confirm loading and error states are announced.
- Confirm note-save messages are announced.
- Confirm drawer/dialog names are announced.
- Confirm decorative logos have empty alternative text.
